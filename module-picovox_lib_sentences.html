(static) quoteSpans(sentences) → {Array.<Array.<SentenceSpan>>}

Sentences' spans - each sentence split at its double quotation marks (curly or straight) into quoted and unquoted spans, the quotations numbered. A quotation runs across sentences until it closes, and a paragraph starts outside one

  • unless its first quotation mark closes one; a speech's paragraph left open continues in the next paragraph's opening quotation (one speaker's quotation). Single quotation marks aren't quotations (the closing one is the apostrophe).
